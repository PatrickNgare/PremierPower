# PremierPower — Product Brief

Marketing + lead-generation website for a licensed residential electrical
contractor operating in Kenya.

## Business

- **Service mix:** residential apartment/house wiring and rewiring of older units.
  Not a general electrician — no commercial fit-outs, no solar, no appliance repair.
- **Market:** Kenya (Nairobi and surrounds). EPRA-licensed; KEBS standards apply.
- **Primary conversion goal:** book a **free site assessment**. There is deliberately
  no self-serve cost estimator — rewiring quotes depend on an on-site inspection,
  so a fake precision range would damage trust rather than build it.
- **Instant-contact channel:** WhatsApp is primary for this market, phone second,
  form third.

## Audience

Homeowners and landlords who are anxious, not price-shopping. The dominant question
in their head is *"is this person licensed and competent, and will bad wiring hurt
my family."* They cannot evaluate wiring quality themselves — it disappears behind
drywall — so the site's job is to make competence legible before anyone visits.

## Positioning

Calm, credible, unmistakably licensed. Explicitly counter the unlicensed-electrician
problem: this client fixes other people's dangerous work.

## Non-goals

- No e-commerce, no booking/scheduling engine, no multi-language.
- No cost estimator (see above).
- No stock photography of generic "electricians" — real installation photos only.

## Content the client must supply before launch

These are hard blockers on the §10 pre-launch checklist. Nothing ships with
placeholder values in these fields:

- [ ] EPRA license number + class (footer, About, near every CTA)
- [ ] Registered business name, physical address, service radius (LocalBusiness NAP)
- [ ] Phone number and WhatsApp number (must be confirmed live)
- [ ] Real installation photos — including panel/DB boards and in-wall wiring
      mid-installation, not just finished rooms
- [ ] Workmanship warranty terms (e.g. "2-year workmanship warranty")
- [ ] 3+ real testimonials, ideally mentioning safety/reliability over aesthetics
- [ ] Insurance details if insured (pairs with "Licensed & Insured" badge)
