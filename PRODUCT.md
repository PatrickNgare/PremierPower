# Premier Power Limited — Product Brief

Marketing and lead-generation website for **Premier Power Limited**, an
electrical engineering service contractor working across Kenya.

Source of truth: the company profile PDF (`PREMIER.pdf`, "PREMIER PROFILE",
March 2026). Where this document and earlier assumptions disagree, the profile
wins.

## Business

- **Positioning (from the profile):** "a leading electrical engineering company
  in the corporate market focusing in providing quality service." Works on
  electrical installation projects all over the country, and is expanding —
  diversifying geographical risk, with further offices planned in Kenya and
  across the border.
- **Sectors undertaken:** residential housing; banks, commercial buildings and
  interiors; office buildings; institutions; hospitality developments, hotels,
  lodges and resorts; industrial projects; health care facilities.
- **Delivery model:** electrical installation with leading contractors in both
  the private and public sectors, coordinating directly with the project
  architect.
- **Registration:** National Construction Authority (NCA), Electrical
  Engineering Service Contractor. Covers electrical installation; electronic
  security, surveillance (CCTV), intruder alarm and access control; lifts,
  hoists, escalators, mechanical ramps, travolators, conveyors and belt
  installation; solar power generation and photovoltaics; compressed air,
  hydraulic, LPG and mechanical gas installation; UPS, AVR and surge
  protection; energy-efficiency retrofitting; construction of power
  transmission lines and installation of power distribution equipment.
- **Vision:** to lead the field of specialisation in **electrical and ICT
  installation** through total quality management.

## Correction history

The first build of this site was **residential-only** and built its trust layer
around an **EPRA licence**. Both were wrong:

- The company works the corporate market across seven sectors; the site had
  said in two places that it does not take on commercial work.
- The credential is NCA registration. EPRA appears nowhere in the client's
  documents — it came from the generic blueprint, not from the client.

Both were corrected once the company profile was read.

carry the specialist systems alongside the electrical package.
